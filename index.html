Listening test

Identify the tones you hear

You will hear short Mandarin syllables. For every item, two syllables are played — choose the tone of each one. You may play each sound up to twice.

  1. 1Pilot Test — a short warm-up
  2. 2Main Test — starts automatically when the pilot is submitted
  • 1 Each item has two syllables: pick a tone for Syllable 1 and one for Syllable 2.
  • 2 The four tones: 1 high level, 2 rising, 3 dipping, 4 falling.
  • 3 Each sound can be played twice at most. Going back does not reset the count.
