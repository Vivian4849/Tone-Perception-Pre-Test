Before you begin

You will hear a series of short Mandarin syllables. For each one, choose the tone you heard.

  • 1 Each item can be played twice at most. The counter beside the player shows how many plays you have left.
  • 2 Choose one of four tones: 1 ˉ mā high level, 2 ˊ má rising, 3 ˇ mǎ dipping, 4 ˋ mà falling.
  • 3 You may go back and change an answer, but play counts do not reset.
  • 4 Keyboard shortcuts: 1 2 3 4 to answer, Space to replay.

Setup (teacher only)

1 · Load audio

Select the sound files for this test, or pick the whole folder. Items are filled in filename order (1, 2, 3 … 10, 11), unless a path is already written in assets/config.js. Files loaded here live only in this browser tab, so repeat this step each session.

2 · Answer key

One value per item, in order, e.g. 1,3,2,4,1. Use 0 for an item that is not scored yet. A key saved here is remembered on this computer; a key written in assets/config.js is used otherwise.

3 · Sending results

Every submission is saved as a CSV on the student's computer. To also collect it centrally, paste your Google Apps Script Web App URL into ENDPOINT at the top of assets/app.js; the page will then POST the same data as JSON.
