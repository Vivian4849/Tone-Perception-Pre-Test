Main Test

The same task as the Pilot Test, with the full set of items. Listen to both syllables, then choose the tone of each one.

  • 1 Each item has two syllables. Choose the tone of Syllable 1, then the tone of Syllable 2 — both before you move on.
  • 2 Each sound can be played twice at most. The counter beside the player shows how many plays are left.
  • 3 The four tones: 1 high level, 2 rising, 3 dipping, 4 falling.
  • 4 You may go back and change an answer, but play counts do not reset. Keyboard: 1–4 to answer, Tab to switch syllable, Space to replay.

Setup (teacher only)

1 · Load audio

Select the sound files for this test, or pick the whole folder. Files are matched in filename order: with two files per item the first pair goes to Syllable 1 and 2 of Item 1; with one file per item that file is used for both syllables. Paths written in assets/config.js take priority. Files loaded here live only in this browser tab.

2 · Answer key

Two values per item, syllable 1 then syllable 2, e.g. 2-4, 1-1, 3-2, 4-4, 1-3. A flat list (2,4,1,1,3,2) also works. Use 0 for a syllable that is not scored yet. A key saved here is remembered on this computer; a key written in assets/config.js is used otherwise.

3 · Sending results

Every submission is saved as a CSV on the student's computer. To also collect it centrally, paste your Google Apps Script Web App URL into ENDPOINT at the top of assets/app.js; the page will then POST the same data as JSON.
